Method

Keep the plan moving.

StreamingWAM amortizes diffusion denoising across control cycles. Instead of discarding the remaining plan after every replan, it rolls a persistent action buffer forward and updates it as new observations arrive.

01

Noise ladder

Future action blocks occupy staggered noise levels, allowing one denoising update per steady-state planning call.

02

Clean action prefix

Recently executed actions condition the next prediction and reduce discontinuities at action-chunk boundaries.

03

Dense visual history

Recent observations provide temporal context for reacting to motion and distribution shifts.
